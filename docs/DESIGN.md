# MorphLab Studio design

The website helps a college/research-demo visitor understand face editing, open a camera without uploads, or produce and compare a photo/video result.

Primary reference: [Runway design analysis](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/voltagent/awesome-design-md/main/design-md/runwayml/DESIGN.md), read from the live VoltAgent catalog on 1 November 2026. Borrow the emphasis on actual media and a quiet interface. Alternative read: [Framer](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/voltagent/awesome-design-md/main/design-md/framer/DESIGN.md); its dense artboards and atmospheric gradients were less suitable for this simple workflow.

The user's Material Design requirement governs color, shape, states, and interaction. MorphLab uses Roboto Variable, teal primary (#28654e), light tonal surfaces (#f5f8f6 / #eaf0ec), rounded controls, large previews, visible focus, and a dark theme. These are MorphLab adaptations, not values attributed to Runway. Local font files are bundled; the Roboto OFL license is retained in third_party.

The design-taste direction was **The product performs**, seed 1503456315. The app draw was **Achromatic ledger**, seed 1471055090; the user-requested Material palette and shapes override its achromatic styling. No direction was redrawn.

On this page, MorphLab's actual Eileen-to-Grace output becomes an interactive before/after demonstration. The live thing is the keyboard- and pointer-operated comparison. The headline and real output share the first screen; the next section selects the actual medium; the last diagram explains identity plus target. Credits link to the original sources rather than implying the pictured people endorse MorphLab.

Studio uses a narrow control column and dominant preview. The phone layout puts the preview first. Advanced settings, hardware details, and research reports remain collapsed. Live frames draw directly to canvas; React updates the summary approximately twice per second. Small route/section entrances use Motion with reduced-motion support; video frames are not animated.

## Flagship refinement

The November refinement preserves Material3/daisyUI5/Roboto and the existing layout. Apple reference (https://getdesign.md/apple/design-md, consulted 2026-11-01) informs spacious product presentation; precise application controls remain task-led. Adapted MorphLab tokens are platinum #F5F6F8 / #EEF0F4, graphite #1F242C and cobalt #244CE8, with charcoal #15171C and pale-blue #ADC2FF in dark mode. These are MorphLab adaptations, not copied brand tokens. Person controls use real unswapped thumbnails; profiler/evaluation detail remains collapsed. Reduced-motion users receive static content with no entrance fade. No decorative camera-loop animations are added.
